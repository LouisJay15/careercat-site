Last updated: 23 November 2026

3. Records available without a request

The following are freely available on this website: our services and prices, our Privacy Notice, our Terms & Conditions and this manual.

4. Records kept under other laws

Where they apply to us, we keep records in terms of the Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011, the Income Tax Act 58 of 1962, the Value-Added Tax Act 89 of 1991, the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 and the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013.

5. Records we hold

  • Client records: intake forms, CVs and other documents clients send us, drafts and final documents, correspondence and session notes.
  • Financial records: invoices, proof of payment, bank statements and tax records.
  • Business records: registration documents and agreements with service providers.
  • Website and marketing records: website content and records of marketing consent.

6. How we process personal information

The purposes for which we process personal information, the categories of people and information involved, who receives it, the transfers we make outside South Africa and how we keep it secure are described in our Privacy Notice. In summary, we hold information about clients and prospective clients, website visitors who accept analytics, and our service providers.

7. How to request a record

  1. Complete Form 2 ("Request for Access to Record"), available on the Information Regulator's website.
  2. Email it to hello@careercat.co.za with "PAIA request" in the subject line, or deliver it to the address above.
  3. Give enough detail for us to identify the record, and say which right you want to protect or exercise with it.

The fees set in the PAIA Regulations may apply. No request fee is payable if you're asking for your own personal information. We'll tell you our decision within 30 days. We may extend this once, by up to 30 days, if the law allows, and will tell you if we do.

8. Grounds for refusing access

We may refuse access only on the grounds set out in Chapter 4 of Part 3 of PAIA. These include protecting other people's privacy, commercial information of third parties, confidential information, the safety of individuals and property, and legally privileged records.

9. If your request is refused

If you're not satisfied with our decision, you can complain to the Information Regulator within 180 days, or apply to a court.

10. Where this manual is available

This manual is available on our website at careercat.co.za/paia.html, and a copy can be requested free of charge by email.
