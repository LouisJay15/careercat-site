2. Our services

We write and improve CVs, LinkedIn profiles, cover letters, personal statements and intro posters; offer 15-minute interview coaching, career counselling and job-search support sessions; and provide proofreading and copywriting. Full descriptions and prices are on our Services & Pricing page.

We are not a recruitment or placement agency. We don't find jobs for you, introduce you to employers or charge you for placement. We can't guarantee interviews, job offers or salary outcomes, because those depend on employers and the job market.

"Career counselling" means practical career guidance. It is not psychological counselling, therapy or psychometric assessment, and it isn't provided by a psychologist registered with the HPCSA.

3. Prices and payment

  • Prices are in South African rand and are shown on our Services & Pricing page. [[VAT_STATUS]]
  • If you buy two or more services together, you get 10% off the total on that invoice.
  • Before any work starts, we'll confirm in writing (by email or WhatsApp) what you're getting, the price and the expected delivery date. That confirmed price applies to your order, even if our website prices change later.
  • Payment is by EFT, using the details on your invoice. Unless we agree otherwise in writing, payment is due before work begins.

4. How an order works

  1. You fill in our intake form, email us or WhatsApp us.
  2. We reply within one business day to discuss what you need.
  3. We confirm the service, price and delivery date in writing, and send an invoice.
  4. Once you've paid, we have a consultation with you and prepare your documents or book your sessions.
  5. We send you a draft, you give feedback, and we finalise it.

5. Corrections and quality

You're entitled to services of good quality, performed in a manner and quality that people are generally entitled to expect (CPA s54). If anything in a draft or final document is inaccurate, or doesn't reflect the information you gave us, tell us and we'll correct it at no extra cost. If we can't put it right, we'll refund a reasonable part of the price, depending on how serious the problem is.

6. Cancellations and refunds

Cooling-off period (ECT Act s44)

Because you buy from us online or by message, you may cancel within 7 days of agreeing to a service, without giving a reason and without penalty. We'll refund what you paid within 30 days of your cancellation. If you asked us to start work within those 7 days, the cooling-off right doesn't apply to work already done (ECT Act s42(2)); the next rule applies instead.

After work has started

You can cancel at any time by letting us know by email or WhatsApp. We'll refund what you paid, less a reasonable charge for the work already done, and we'll show you how we worked it out. Any work we've completed by then is yours to keep.

Coaching session bundles

If you cancel a 5-session bundle, we'll refund the sessions you haven't used, less the difference between the bundle price and the single-session price for the sessions you did use. To move a session, let us know before it starts and we'll find another time.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are paid by EFT to the account you paid from, within 30 days.

7. Your part

  • Give us accurate and complete information. We'll write the strongest honest version of your story, but we won't include qualifications, experience or results that aren't true.
  • Check your final documents before you use them. You're responsible for what you send to employers.
  • Get permission from anyone whose details you include, such as references.

8. Your documents

Once you've paid in full, the documents we write for you are yours to use, change and share as you like. We won't use your documents, name or story as an example or testimonial without your written permission.

10. Our responsibility to you

We'll provide our services with reasonable care and skill. We're not responsible for decisions made by employers or recruiters, or for losses caused by information you gave us that was incorrect. Apart from your rights under the CPA, and except in cases of gross negligence or fraud, our total responsibility for any claim relating to a service is limited to the price you paid for that service.

11. Complaints

If you're unhappy with anything we've done, email hello@careercat.co.za or WhatsApp us, and we'll try to put it right. If we can't resolve it together, you can contact:

  • the Consumer Goods and Services Ombud: cgso.org.za
  • the National Consumer Commission: thencc.org.za
  • for privacy complaints, the Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za
