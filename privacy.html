2. What we collect

We collect information directly from you when you fill in our intake form, email us, message us on WhatsApp or take part in a coaching session:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, city or province and how you prefer to be contacted.
  • Career information: your employment status, experience, industry, job titles, the roles you are targeting, your background and job-search challenges.
  • Your CV and documents: anything in the CV or other documents you send us, such as work history, qualifications and references.
  • Records of your consent and of the services you ask for.
  • Messages and notes: emails and WhatsApp messages you send us, and notes we make during coaching sessions.
  • Payment records: invoices and proof of payment. We do not collect card details on this website.
  • Website data: our web host keeps basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics also records how you use the site (see Cookies and analytics).

We don't buy personal information or collect it about you from other people. The only information about other people we hold is what you choose to put in your documents, such as your references' contact details. Please make sure they're happy for you to share them.

3. Sensitive information in CVs

CVs sometimes include "special personal information" as defined in section 26 of POPIA. Examples are race or ethnic origin (often listed for employment-equity purposes), health or disability, religion, trade-union membership and criminal record. We process this kind of information only:

  • with your consent, which you give on our intake form (section 27(1)(a) of POPIA);
  • where you have chosen to include it; and
  • to prepare and improve your documents. We never use it to make decisions about you.

You don't need to send us your ID number. Please remove it from your CV before uploading, unless you want it to appear on your final CV.

Under 18? A parent or guardian must complete the intake form with you, or give consent on your behalf (section 35 of POPIA).

5. Do you have to give it to us?

No. Giving us personal information is voluntary. However, we need the fields marked with an asterisk (*) on the intake form to reply to you, and we can't write your documents or coach you without information about your career. No law requires you to give us your information, apart from the details we need to issue a valid invoice.

7. Information sent outside South Africa

Some of the providers above store or process information outside South Africa, mainly in the United States. Under section 72 of POPIA, we transfer your information to them because the transfer is needed to provide the service you asked for, and with your consent. These providers are bound by their own data-protection terms and security commitments. If you would rather not use them, email us your CV directly or contact us by phone, and we'll agree another way to work.

8. How long we keep it

  • Intake details, CVs, drafts, final documents and session notes: up to 5 years after we last worked together. This lets us update your documents quickly if you come back. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time, and we will.
  • Invoices and payment records: for as long as tax law requires, generally 5 years (Tax Administration Act 28 of 2011).
  • Your opt-in for tips and updates: until you opt out.

When we no longer need information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

9. How we protect it

We keep your information in password-protected accounts that only The Career Cat can access. We use providers that encrypt data in transit, and this website is served only over HTTPS. We share information only with the people and providers who need it to deliver your service. If we believe your information has been accessed by someone without authority, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as soon as reasonably possible, as section 22 of POPIA requires.

10. Your POPIA rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your information: ask whether we hold information about you and get a copy, free of charge (POPIA s23).
  • Correct or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete or that we're no longer allowed to keep (s24).
  • Object to us using your information on the basis of our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time (s11(3)).
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. This doesn't affect anything we did lawfully before you withdrew it (s11(2)(b)).
  • Not be subject to automated decisions. We don't make any decisions about you by automated means (s71).
  • Complain to the Information Regulator (s74).

To use any of these rights, email hello@careercat.co.za with "POPIA request" in the subject line. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, and we'll respond within 30 days. Formal requests for records can also be made under our PAIA Manual.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Regulator:

11. Cookies and analytics

Right now this website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other websites. Our fonts and images are served from our own domain.

If we switch on Google Analytics to see how people find and use the site, you'll see a banner asking for your permission first. Analytics cookies (named _ga and _ga_…, kept for up to 2 years) are only set if you press "Accept analytics". If you press "Decline", nothing is loaded. You can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer, which appears once analytics is switched on.

12. Tips and updates

We only send career tips and service updates if you tick the optional box on the intake form. Every message tells you how to stop them. You can also reply "STOP" on WhatsApp or "unsubscribe" by email at any time.

13. Changes to this notice

We'll update this notice if the way we handle personal information changes, and change the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information we already hold, we'll let you know before it takes effect.
